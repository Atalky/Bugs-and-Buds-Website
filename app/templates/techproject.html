Injection Guard

In progress

Python · pandas · scikit-learn · Ollama (phi3:mini) · PydanticAI · FastAPI · Docker · AWS

Automated prompt-injection testing for LLM agents. It attacks a small local model with injection attempts to measure how often it leaks a secret hidden in its instructions, then tests whether a trained detector in front of it reduces that, with every result reported alongside a confidence interval. The detector will be served as a tested, containerised API on AWS. Started 30 September 2026.
